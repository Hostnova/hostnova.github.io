## 🎯 KEY RESEARCH INSIGHTS

### Global Marketplace Best Practices
- **Trust-First Design:** All successful platforms prioritize verification and social proof
- **Mobile-First Approach:** 80%+ of service bookings happen on mobile devices
- **Local Adaptation:** Regional platforms outperform global ones through cultural customization
- **AI-Powered Matching:** Smart recommendations increase conversion by 35-50%
- **Transparent Pricing:** Upfront pricing transparency builds trust and reduces friction

### Kenya-Specific Design Requirements
- **Data-Light Design:** Optimize for 2G/3G networks with intermittent connectivity
- **M-Pesa Integration:** Primary payment method for Kenyan market
- **Community Trust:** Leverage social networks and local testimonials
- **Bilingual Approach:** English primary with strategic Swahili integration
- **Cultural Symbols:** Integrate Kenyan identity without overwhelming professional appearance

### Ultra-Modern Design Trends (2025)
- **Neumorphism 2.0:** Subtle 3D effects with improved accessibility
- **Variable Typography:** Dynamic fonts that adapt to screen and context
- **Micro-Interactions:** Purposeful animation for user feedback and engagement
- **Voice Interface Integration:** Speech-to-text search and commands
- **AR Service Previews:** Augmented reality for home services visualization
