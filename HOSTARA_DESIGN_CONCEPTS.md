## 🎯 CONVERSION-FOCUSED ELEMENTS

### Call-to-Action Hierarchy
1. **Primary CTA:** App Download (Orange gradient button)
2. **Secondary CTA:** Service Booking (Orange outline button)
3. **Tertiary CTA:** Provider Signup (Text link with icon)

### Trust Signal Placement
1. **Header:** Provider count and verification badges
2. **Hero:** Service completion stats and ratings
3. **Categories:** Popular service indicators
4. **Providers:** Ratings, reviews, and response times
5. **Footer:** Security badges and compliance info

### Social Proof Integration
1. **Live Activity:** "3 services booked in the last hour"
2. **Popular Choices:** "Most requested this week"
3. **Local Testimonials:** Real customer reviews with photos
4. **Provider Success:** "John has completed 127 jobs"
5. **Community Size:** "Join 10,000+ satisfied customers"
