Platform Features

Everything You Need to Succeed

Hostara provides powerful tools and features designed specifically for the Kenyan market, helping businesses of all sizes thrive in the digital economy.

Mobile-First Design

Our Flutter-based mobile app ensures seamless performance on both Android and iOS devices, optimized for Kenyan users.

M-Pesa Integration

Secure payment processing through M-Pesa Daraja API, enabling direct business-to-customer transactions.

Multi-Role System

Support for 6 distinct user roles including guests, clients, agents, managers, and administrators.

Business Tiers

Flexible pricing tiers from Student (KES 1,000) to Enterprise (KES 10,000) setup, designed for every business size.

Campus Focus

Special features for university campuses and student entrepreneurs across Kenya's major institutions.

Real-Time Analytics

Comprehensive business analytics, order tracking, and performance insights to help grow your business.
