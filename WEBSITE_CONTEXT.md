4. **Contact Form Submissions** - Sales inquiries

## 🚀 **PLATFORM OVERVIEW - HOSTARA BY HOSTNOVA**

### **Core Concept**
Hostara is an **"Uber for Business Services"** platform designed for the Kenyan market, enabling businesses to host their services online while customers discover and interact with these services seamlessly.

### **Key Differentiators**
- **Kenya-First:** Built specifically for Kenyan market needs
- **M-Pesa Integration:** Direct payment flows via M-Pesa Daraja API
- **Tier-Based Model:** Accessible to students through enterprise businesses
- **Multi-Service:** Food delivery, laundry, tech repair, freelance, and more
- **Commission-Based:** Businesses keep payments, platform earns setup + monthly + per-client fees

### **Market Position**
- **Primary Market:** Nairobi, expanding to other Kenyan cities
- **Secondary Focus:** University campuses (UoN, JKUAT, etc.)
- **Unique Value:** "Host Your Hustle" - empowering Kenyan entrepreneurs

## 👥 **USER ROLES - 6 TYPES**

### **1. Guest User (7-day trial)**
- Browse services, view businesses
- Cannot place orders (redirected to register)
- Temporary data migrates on registration

### **2. Client (Customer)**
- Full ordering capabilities
- M-Pesa and COD payments
- Order tracking and history

### **3. Agent_Employee**
- Limited business operations
- Process orders for employer
- Update delivery status

### **4. Agent_Owner (Business Owner)**
- Full business management
- Configure services and pricing
- Manage employees and analytics
- **PRIMARY TARGET FOR WEBSITE**

### **5. Manager (Regional IT Support)**
- Handle technical issues
- Support Agent_Owners in area

### **6. Admin (Platform Control)**
- Full system management
- Configure M-Pesa and platform settings
