User Regulations and Platform Guidelines

Last Updated: January 1, 2024

Important Notice: These User Regulations complement our Terms and Conditions and are designed to ensure a safe, professional, and compliant marketplace for all users in accordance with Kenyan laws and regulations.

1. User Categories and Responsibilities

1.1 Service Seekers (Customers)

Users seeking services through the Hostara platform must:

  • Provide accurate service requirements and expectations
  • Communicate respectfully with service providers
  • Pay agreed fees promptly and in full
  • Provide honest reviews and feedback
  • Report any issues or concerns promptly

1.2 Service Providers

Users offering services through the platform must:

  • Maintain all required licenses and certifications
  • Provide services of professional quality
  • Communicate transparently about pricing and timelines
  • Comply with all applicable Kenyan laws and regulations
  • Maintain appropriate insurance where required
  • Complete background verification processes

2. Prohibited Conduct

Violation of these regulations may result in account suspension or termination.

2.1 Illegal Activities

Users are strictly prohibited from:

  • Engaging in any illegal activities under Kenyan law
  • Money laundering or financial crimes
  • Tax evasion or fraudulent financial practices
  • Copyright or trademark infringement
  • Sale of illegal goods or services
  • Pyramid schemes or multi-level marketing

2.2 Platform Abuse

  • Creating fake accounts or profiles
  • Manipulating reviews or ratings
  • Spamming or unsolicited communications
  • Attempting to circumvent platform fees
  • Using the platform for purposes other than intended

2.3 Harmful Behavior

  • Harassment, threats, or intimidation
  • Discrimination based on protected characteristics
  • Sharing inappropriate or offensive content
  • Violating others' privacy or personal information
  • Engaging in predatory behavior

3. Service Quality Standards

3.1 Professional Standards

All service providers must maintain:

  • Minimum 4.0-star average rating (after 10+ reviews)
  • Response time within 24 hours for inquiries
  • Professional communication and conduct
  • Timely completion of agreed services
  • Compliance with industry best practices

3.2 Verification Requirements

Service providers must complete:

  • Identity verification (National ID or Passport)
  • Professional qualification verification (where applicable)
  • Business registration verification (for businesses)
  • Background check (for certain service categories)
  • Skills assessment (category-specific)

4. Financial Regulations and Taxation

4.1 Tax Compliance

All users must comply with Kenyan tax obligations:

  • Service providers must register for appropriate tax categories
  • Income earned through the platform must be declared to KRA
  • VAT registration required for eligible service providers
  • Proper record keeping of all transactions

4.2 Payment Processing

  • All payments must be processed through approved channels
  • No cash transactions are permitted for safety reasons
  • Platform fees are automatically deducted from payments
  • Refunds processed according to our dispute resolution policy

Tax Advisory: We recommend consulting with a qualified tax professional to ensure compliance with all Kenyan Revenue Authority requirements.

5. Data Protection and Privacy

5.1 Personal Data Handling

Users must respect data protection laws:

  • Only collect personal data necessary for service provision
  • Obtain consent before processing personal information
  • Protect client data with appropriate security measures
  • Delete data when no longer needed
  • Report data breaches within 72 hours

5.2 Communication Privacy

  • All communications must remain professional
  • No sharing of personal contact information without consent
  • Platform messaging system must be used for initial contact
  • Recording of calls/meetings requires explicit consent

6. Enforcement and Penalties

Violation Level Examples First Offense Repeat Offense
Minor Late responses, minor service delays Warning + guidance Temporary restriction
Moderate Poor service quality, inappropriate communication Account restriction (7-30 days) Account suspension (30-90 days)
Severe Fraud, harassment, illegal activities Immediate account suspension Permanent account termination

7. Reporting and Dispute Resolution

7.1 Reporting Violations

Users can report violations through:

  • In-app reporting system
  • Email: violations@hostnova.com
  • 24/7 support hotline: +254 (0) 700 000 000
  • Online form on our website

7.2 Investigation Process

  1. Initial report review (24-48 hours)
  2. Evidence collection and user notification
  3. Investigation and fact-finding
  4. Decision and penalty implementation
  5. Appeal process (if applicable)

7.3 Appeals Process

Users may appeal decisions by:

  • Submitting an appeal within 14 days of the decision
  • Providing additional evidence or clarification
  • Requesting mediation through approved mediators
  • Escalating to external dispute resolution bodies

8. Platform Safety and Security

8.1 Account Security

Users must:

  • Use strong, unique passwords
  • Enable two-factor authentication
  • Keep login credentials confidential
  • Report suspicious activities immediately
  • Log out from shared devices

8.2 Meeting Safety Guidelines

  • Meet in public places for initial consultations
  • Inform trusted contacts about service appointments
  • Use platform messaging for service communication
  • Trust your instincts and report concerns
  • Follow COVID-19 and health guidelines

9. Intellectual Property Protection

9.1 Content Ownership

Users must:

  • Only upload content they own or have permission to use
  • Respect copyrights, trademarks, and patents
  • Provide proper attribution when required
  • Report intellectual property violations

9.2 Work Product Rights

  • Clear agreement on intellectual property ownership
  • Respect client confidentiality and trade secrets
  • Proper licensing of software and tools used
  • Attribution requirements for creative work

10. Regulatory Compliance

10.1 Industry-Specific Requirements

Service providers in regulated industries must comply with relevant authorities:

  • Healthcare: Kenya Medical Practitioners and Dentists Council
  • Legal Services: Law Society of Kenya
  • Financial Services: Central Bank of Kenya
  • Construction: National Construction Authority
  • Education: Teachers Service Commission
  • Transport: National Transport and Safety Authority

10.2 Business Licensing

Where required, service providers must maintain:

  • Valid business permits from county governments
  • Trade licenses for specific business types
  • Professional body memberships
  • Environmental compliance certificates
  • Fire safety certificates (where applicable)

11. Updates and Amendments

These regulations may be updated to:

  • Reflect changes in Kenyan law
  • Address emerging platform issues
  • Improve user safety and experience
  • Align with industry best practices

Users will be notified of significant changes at least 30 days in advance.
